# Every stat we hold or can fetch, and who reads it

- **One source per figure per screen.** Fantrax's counts never sit beside FPL's count of the same thing, and `FPts` only ever means the served league's Fantrax points. Current exceptions, both labelled on screen: the player's match log (`players/[fantraxId]/MatchLog.tsx`) prints FPL's account left of a rule and Fantrax's right of it, FPL's points headed `FPL`; Data › Teams (`players/teams/teamRows.ts`) sets Fantrax's points, keeper clean sheets and goals against beside FPL's expected figures. The pool's `FPts` prices a man at his listed position, not the slot he fills.
- **Reading a table.** One row per stat. *Domain field* names `Type.field` at a repo path and the line where those fields begin. *Read by* is grouped screens · paper · derivations · scripts, with paths relative to `apps/companion/app/` unless they start `core:` (`packages/core/src/`) or `scripts/`; it names who reads the field itself, and a file that only fetches or passes it on says so.
- **What the test holds.** `scripts/stats-reference.test.ts` fails when a cited line is no longer the first declaration of the fields named before it, when a stat type gains a count, flag or keyed bag of figures with no row, when a stat written as text or a list loses its row, or when the stats league's columns leave the probe's order. It cannot check a Completeness fraction or a Read-by list: those are records, re-counted by hand.
- **What the tables cover.** Every field our types carry. FPL's bootstrap sends 75 keys we never type; *FPL bootstrap keys never typed* lists them by family, so nobody re-probes for season cards or FPL's prices.
- **Completeness** is the fraction somebody counted and the day they counted it; `uncounted` means nobody has. The counts come from `.claude/rules/providers.md`, `docs/record/PLATFORM_NOTES.md` and the `raw.ts` comments.
- **Grain**: count, rate, per-90, derived or projection; then match, gameweek, season, career or live. A gameweek figure on a double gameweek covers two matches.
- **Glossary.** *code*: FPL's season-stable player or fixture key, the only one persisted. *elementId*: FPL's per-season player id (`id`), never persisted. *fantraxId*: Fantrax's five-character player id. *club*: a real Premier League side. *team*: a fantasy side in our league. *gameweek*: FPL's round of fixtures. *period*: Fantrax's scoring window, numbered with the gameweeks but on its own dates. *Round*: the gameweek as the app speaks of it. *fixture*: one real match. *matchup*: one pairing of two teams in one period. *fetchedAt*: when a snapshot was read, injected so mappers stay pure.
- The stats league's columns are listed for what they would give us; nothing reads that league yet.
