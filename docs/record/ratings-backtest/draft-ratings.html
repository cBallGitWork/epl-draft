What changed from your feedback

  • Strengths: the sister model was too smoothed (Wolves' defence read as average while conceding 1.79 a game). Ours is goals and xG so far this season against the league's, eased in over six games. It tracks the season's goals at 0.92 for attack and 0.9 for defence, against 0.8 and 0.81 before.
  • Scale: a blank is now 4–5; across the season 1 match reaches 10 and 5 reach 1.
  • Fantrax points: the real league's own points from Fantrax for 7,937 matches. The other 2,584 are men Fantrax no longer lists (relegated or gone abroad), priced by the league's own rules, which reproduce Fantrax's points exactly in 98.3% of matches and within a point in 99.9%.
  • Points lead: mark and league points correlate at 0.943.
  • Being terrible: a big chance missed now costs a point before the table, an error leading to a goal two.
  • Underlying is gone: what a man will do next is the projections' job, so the mark is the match alone.
  • Haaland stays 7th: good, a little under what was expected of him.
  • The real league's scoring, in full: every card now shows his league points by category, DefCon and keeper points included. Departed keepers were short: their keeper points now count saves, punches and high claims as Fantrax does, and the league's rules match Fantrax exactly in 98.3% of matches.
